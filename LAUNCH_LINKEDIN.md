# SettleIQ — Open-Source Financial Reconciliation Platform

We're launching SettleIQ, an open-source financial reconciliation platform for finance teams and developers.

## The Problem

Every month, finance teams spend days reconciling payment transactions against bank deposits using spreadsheets. Existing tools (BlackLine, HighRadius, Modern Treasury) are expensive, proprietary, and hide their matching logic behind black boxes.

## The Solution

SettleIQ is an open-source reconciliation engine that:

- **Matches payments, payouts, and bank deposits** using exact decimal arithmetic (no float errors)
- **Classifies 7 types of discrepancies** — amount breaks, timing breaks, phantom records, ambiguous matches, and more
- **Provides visual transaction traces** showing the complete money journey from charge to bank deposit
- **Offers AI-backed investigation hypotheses** with supporting and contradicting evidence
- **Maintains a hash-chained audit journal** for tamper-evident record-keeping
- **Exports results as JSON or CSV**
- **Provides a full REST API** for developers to integrate reconciliation into their applications
