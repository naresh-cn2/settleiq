# SettleIQ — Open-source financial reconciliation platform

## Tagline
Open-source reconciliation engine for finance teams and developers

## Description

SettleIQ helps finance teams reconcile payment transactions, processor payouts, and bank deposits — without spreadsheets or expensive enterprise software.

**What it does:**
- Exact decimal arithmetic (no float errors)
- Constituent-level payout decomposition
- 7 types of discrepancy detection
- Visual transaction traces
- Evidence-backed investigation hypotheses
- Hash-chained audit journal
- JSON and CSV export
- Full REST API

**Try the public demo:** https://settleiq-zeta.vercel.app

**GitHub:** https://github.com/naresh-cn2/settleiq

**Pricing:** Free tier available. Paid plans from $49/month.

## Who is it for?

Finance operations teams, developers building reconciliation into their apps, accountants and auditors, and small businesses processing payments via Stripe, bank transfers, etc.
